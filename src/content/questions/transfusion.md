:::check peds #irradiate-digeorge sec=products refs=bsh-neonatal-2016
A newborn with interrupted aortic arch and hypocalcemia (suspected 22q11.2 deletion) needs RBCs before cardiac surgery. Which product modification is essential?
- [ ] Washed only
- [x] Irradiated (plus leukoreduced/CMV-safe)
- [ ] Volume-reduced only
- [ ] None; standard units are fine
> Suspected T-cell immunodeficiency (DiGeorge) is an indication for irradiated cellular products to prevent TA-GVHD, until immune status is known. Use fresh units for large volumes in neonates.

:::check both #iga-anaphylaxis sec=reactions refs=nathan-oski
Within 2 minutes of starting plasma, a patient develops stridor, hypotension and wheeze, with no fever. Prior reactions were similar. Which test is most relevant for future product selection?
- [ ] DAT
- [x] Serum IgA level and anti-IgA antibodies (and haptoglobin in some ancestries)
- [ ] Blood cultures of the bag
- [ ] BNP
> Anaphylactic transfusion reactions without fever suggest antibodies to plasma proteins. Severe IgA deficiency with anti-IgA calls for IgA-deficient plasma or washed cellular products.
:::

Two hours into a second unit, an 82-year-old with heart failure becomes dyspneic. BP 172/96, JVD, bilateral crackles, NT-proBNP rising. What is the treatment?
- [ ] Supportive care only; avoid diuretics (TRALI)
- [x] Stop transfusion, sit upright, oxygen, IV diuretic
- [ ] Epinephrine
- [ ] Broad-spectrum antibiotics
> Hypertension, JVD and rising BNP within 12 h point to TACO. Prevent it with single-unit transfusions, slower rates and diuretics in at-risk patients.

:::check both #septic-plt sec=reactions
Twenty minutes into a platelet transfusion, a patient has rigors, T 39.8 °C (from 37.0 °C) and BP 82/44. What is the most important step besides stopping the transfusion and resuscitating?
- [ ] Antihistamine and resume
- [x] Culture the patient and the platelet bag, start broad-spectrum antibiotics, and notify the blood bank to quarantine co-components
- [ ] Give acetaminophen and continue more slowly
- [ ] Order a DAT only
> Platelets are stored at room temperature and are the product most often bacterially contaminated. High fever with rigors and hypotension is a septic reaction until proven otherwise.

:::check peds #volume-calc sec=peds-dosing refs=bsh-neonatal-2016
A 20-kg child with symptomatic anemia has Hb 6.0 g/dL. You aim for ~9.0 g/dL. About what RBC volume is needed (factor ≈ 4)?
- [ ] 80 mL
- [ ] 120 mL
- [x] 240 mL
- [ ] 480 mL
> Volume = ΔHb × weight × 4 = 3 × 20 × 4 = 240 mL (~12 mL/kg). Infuse over 3–4 h (~5 mL/kg/h) unless the child is unstable.
:::

:::check both #ta-gvhd sec=products
Ten days after cardiac surgery, an adult develops fever, rash, diarrhea, abnormal LFTs and pancytopenia. During surgery he received a non-irradiated unit directed from his son. What is the diagnosis?
- [ ] Acute GVHD after HSCT
- [x] Transfusion-associated GVHD
- [ ] Delayed hemolytic transfusion reaction
- [ ] Drug reaction with eosinophilia (DRESS)
> HLA haplotype sharing with first-degree relatives lets donor lymphocytes engraft. Pancytopenia (marrow attack) distinguishes TA-GVHD, which is > 90% fatal. Directed donations from relatives must be irradiated.
:::

:::check peds #picu-threshold sec=rbc-thresholds refs=tripicu,aabb-rbc-2023
A hemodynamically stable 7-year-old in the PICU with pneumonia has Hb 7.4 g/dL. No cyanotic heart disease, SCD or active bleeding. What should you do?
- [ ] Transfuse 15 mL/kg to Hb > 10
- [x] No transfusion; restrictive threshold of 7 g/dL
- [ ] Transfuse if lactate is normal
- [ ] Give erythropoietin
> TRIPICU and AABB 2023 support a 7 g/dL threshold in stable critically ill children. It is as safe as higher thresholds and halves transfusion exposure.
:::

:::check adult #cirrhosis-ffp sec=plasma-cryo refs=aga-cirrhosis-2019
A non-bleeding patient with cirrhosis (INR 1.8, platelets 78 × 10⁹/L) needs a paracentesis. What is appropriate?
- [ ] 4 units of plasma to correct INR < 1.5
- [ ] Platelet transfusion to > 100 × 10⁹/L
- [x] Proceed without prophylactic plasma
- [ ] Vitamin K 10 mg IV and delay 24 h
> INR doesn't predict procedural bleeding in cirrhosis (rebalanced hemostasis). Prophylactic plasma adds volume and risk without benefit for low-risk procedures like paracentesis.
:::
