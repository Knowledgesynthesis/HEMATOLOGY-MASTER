:::check peds #burkitt-tls sec=nhl-peds refs=inter-b-nhl
A 7-year-old with an abdominal mass has stage III Burkitt lymphoma. Uric acid 9.8 mg/dL, K 5.4 mmol/L, creatinine rising. G6PD is normal. What is the key preventive step before chemotherapy?
- [ ] Allopurinol alone and wait 48 h
- [x] Hyperhydration without potassium plus rasburicase, with close electrolyte monitoring
- [ ] Urine alkalinization with bicarbonate
- [ ] Delay chemotherapy until uric acid normalizes
> Burkitt is the highest-risk tumor for TLS. Rasburicase lowers uric acid within hours. Alkalinization is no longer recommended (calcium phosphate precipitation). Start chemotherapy promptly with monitoring and dialysis readiness.
:::

:::check adult #malt sec=nhl-adult refs=who-haem5-lymphoid
Endoscopy shows localized gastric MALT lymphoma with *H. pylori* gastritis and no t(11;18). What is the first-line treatment?
- [ ] R-CHOP
- [x] *H. pylori* eradication therapy with endoscopic follow-up
- [ ] Total gastrectomy
- [ ] Radiation only
> Most localized *H. pylori*-positive gastric MALT lymphomas regress after eradication. t(11;18) predicts resistance. Radiation is used for persistent disease.
:::

:::check peds #hl-survivor sec=hodgkin refs=ahod1331
A 26-year-old woman had mediastinal radiation for Hodgkin lymphoma at age 16. When should breast cancer surveillance begin?
- [ ] Age 40 mammography, like the general population
- [ ] Age 50
- [x] Now: annual mammography plus breast MRI starting at age 25 or 8 years after radiation, whichever is later
- [ ] Only if symptomatic
> Chest radiation in adolescence carries a breast cancer risk similar to BRCA carriers. COG survivorship guidelines start annual MRI plus mammography at 25 or 8 years post-radiation, whichever is later.
:::

:::check adult #slim sec=myeloma refs=imwg-2014
A 58-year-old has 3.4 g/dL IgG-κ, 68% clonal marrow plasma cells, and normal calcium, creatinine and Hb, with no lytic lesions. What is the classification?
- [ ] MGUS
- [ ] Smoldering myeloma
- [x] Multiple myeloma (SLiM criterion met)
- [ ] Plasma cell leukemia
> ≥ 60% clonal plasma cells is a myeloma-defining biomarker (SLiM). Treat it as myeloma even without CRAB features, because progression risk is ~80% within 2 years.
:::

:::check adult #wm-hyperviscosity sec=al-amyloid refs=who-haem5-lymphoid
A 69-year-old with an IgM paraprotein of 5.8 g/dL has headache, blurred vision, epistaxis and retinal "sausage" veins. What is the immediate treatment?
- [ ] High-dose dexamethasone
- [x] Plasmapheresis
- [ ] Red cell transfusion
- [ ] Rituximab alone
> Symptomatic hyperviscosity in Waldenström is an emergency. Plasmapheresis rapidly removes IgM. Rituximab alone can cause an IgM flare, so start definitive therapy after or with plasmapheresis.
:::
